Manual demo video required

TikTok rejects AI agents, automated tools, and screen-simulation demos. Record with Windows Game Bar (Win+G) or similar while you click through the real app.

  1. Open Chrome with address bar visible → go to https://legenda89.github.io/ClipMaker/ (full URL must be readable).
  2. Show the hero: brand Clipmaker, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.
  3. Open the Clipmaker Windows desktop app (window title / UI shows Clipmaker).
  4. Login Kit: Connect TikTok → approve OAuth → return to app (user.info.basic).
  5. Import audio + cover → create/render a short clip (show the real UI).
  6. video.upload: choose Inbox / draft mode → Publish → success (or inbox confirmation).
  7. video.publish: choose Direct post → Publish → success (Sandbox may be private / SELF_ONLY).
  8. Keep the recording under ~50 MB (mp4/mov). Do not show code or slide decks.

Upload that MP4 in App review. Do not upload the old simulated demo.
